Denise Laroze

Faculty Member

Denise Laroze is an Associate Professor at the Research Center for Social Complexity (CICS) of the School of Government at Universidad del Desarrollo. She holds a Ph.D. in Government (Political Science) from the University of Essex and an M.Sc. in Political Science and Political Economy from the London School of Economics, United Kingdom. She currently leads the Mobile Experimental Laboratory within the PoliCICS group.

Her academic research specializes in behavioral science and decision-making. Her work lies in the field of experimental and behavioral economics, favoring experimental methods combined with advanced econometrics. Her main areas of study include cooperation decisions in social dilemmas and the architecture of personal financial decisions, such as pensions and credit cards.

She is currently Principal Investigator of Fondecyt Regular 1261329 (2026-2030), a project that evaluates how identity and its associated expectations influence sustainable harvesting decisions in social dilemmas.

Her work includes collaborations with institutions such as Chile’s Ministry of Economy, the Superintendency of Pensions, SERNAC (the national consumer protection agency) and Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA). With them she has led empirical research that has had a direct impact on the design of public policy.
