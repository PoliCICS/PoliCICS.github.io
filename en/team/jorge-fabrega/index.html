Jorge Fábrega

PoliCICS Director

Jorge Fábrega is an Associate Professor and researcher at the Research Center for Social Complexity (CICS) of the School of Government at Universidad del Desarrollo. He holds degrees in Business and Economics (1996) and in Sociology (1998) from the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile, and earned his Ph.D. in Public Policy from the University of Chicago in 2009.

From 2018 to 2022 he directed the Ph.D. Program in Social Complexity Sciences at Universidad del Desarrollo, where he currently teaches Political Economy and Social Network Theory. He also directs the Political Behavior Lab (PoliCICS) at CICS.

His research lies mainly at the intersection of political economy, political behavior and social network analysis. His work studies how institutional incentives and interaction structures shape individual decisions and collective outcomes, with particular attention to political representation, polarization and cooperation. His research interests also include crime and recidivism.

More recently, he has developed a line of research on human cognition and artificial intelligence, focused on how people delegate cognitive tasks, evaluate the responses of these systems and integrate their outputs into decision-making. This agenda examines the conditions that preserve human autonomy, control and accountability when interacting with artificial intelligence systems.
